#### Main Standalone Note 1
# What Is It

- Kitchen-based [[egocentric]] videos
- interconnected ground-truth labels
    - recipe steps
    - actions
    - ingredients /w nutritional values
    - moving objects
    - audio annotations
- all annotations grounded in 3D through **digital twinning** of the scene, fixtures, object locations, and primed with gaze
- recordings in diverse home environments
- first dataset collected in-the-wild but with detailed annotations matching those in controlled lab environments
- Details:
    
    - **41 hours of video**
    - **9 Kitchens with digital twins of 413 kitchen features**
    - **69 recipes**
    - **59K fine-grained actions**
    - **51K audio events**
    - **20K object movements**
    - **37K object masks lifted to 3D**
    - **On average, 263 annotations per minute of unscripted videos**
- On average recipes contained
    
    - 6.6 steps
    - 8.1 ingredients
    - took 4 hours 48 minutes across 2.1 videos from preparation to serving
    - longest recipe took 2 days and 6 hours to complete
